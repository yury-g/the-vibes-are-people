# A visual vocabulary for making

The next step after attribution is agency: teachers, students, artists, makers and their AI tools should be able to describe an aesthetic choice and understand which parameters and methods can change it.

The first working interface at `/vocabulary/` separates observed qualities (flow, cohesion, interference, branching, grain, subdivision), possible mechanisms, and situated interpretations. Density, scale and irregularity change original illustrative exercises. A copyable brief connects those choices to the site's sourced recipe contributions. Interpretations are questions rather than universal sign-to-meaning rules. The vocabulary does not assign an aesthetic quality to a sole inventor.

This is a pilot semiotic vocabulary, not a finished taxonomy or a reconstruction of original artworks. Future work: richer perceptual axes, comparisons across contexts and media, classroom observations, coauthored descriptions from practitioners, and links between folk terminology and technical methods. Keep unrecorded experiments and oral histories visible as attributed leads.
